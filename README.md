Project investigating the normative scaling of personalized functional brain networks (PFNS) as well as the effect of the 22q11.2 deletion on PFNs, above and beyond scaling.

Normative data were obtained from the Philadelphia Neurodevelopmental Cohort (PNC) and 22q11DS data from a Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia cohort; imaging in both cohorts was acquired on the same scanner. From these data, we constructed: 1) a normative dataset composed of PNC individuals (n=1332, ages 8-23) and 2) a 22q11DS matched sample including 62 cases (ages 10-26) and 152 controls from PNC matched on age, sex, race, and median FD.

All PNC and 22q11DS participant MRI scans were acquired on the same 3T scanner with the same acquisition parameters. Scans included a magnetization-prepared, rapid acquisition gradient-echo T1-weighted (MPRAGE) image as well as both resting-state (6:18 minutes) and emotional-identification task (10:36 minutes) fMRI scans. All fMRI scans were processed using the same software pipeline of fMRIPrep 25.0.0 and XCP-D 0.10.6. In both datasets, 17 PFNs were derived from concatenated rest and task fMRI data (maximum 16:54 minutes) using spatially-regularized non-negative matrix factorization via the pNet toolbox.

Step 1. Scripts related to functional and structural imaging data curation, pre- and post-processing, and PFN derivation via pNet.

Step 2. Scripts related to imaging QC.

Step 3. Scripts related to preparing the various imaging phenotypes used for analysis downstream: functional network area, anatomical network area, PFN vertex loadings, functional network extent

Step 4. Scripts related to filtered participants based on QC and data availability, as well as demographic matching between 22q and PNC samples

Step 5. Network-level models investigating normative scaling of PFNs (functional and anatomical network areas)

Step 6. Network-level models investigating 22q11DS effects on PFNs (functional and anatomical network areas)

Step 7. Vertex-level models investigating normative scaling of PFNs (PFN vertex loadings)

Step 8. Vertex-level models investigating 22q11DS effects on PFNs (PFN vertex loadings)
